    Terms and consent
    1. Copyright. You, the contributor ("you"), retain all copyright in the submitted work.
    2. Purpose. The organizers will use the work solely for non-commercial research and educational purposes.
    3. Restrictions. The organizers will not sell the work, use it in commercial advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence models or to build datasets for that purpose.
    4. Risks. The meet-up is part of CHI 2027, where attendees may photograph and record sessions, so your work may be photographed or recorded there. Any work shown in person or online can also be copied by others. To reduce this, the organizers will keep each work's QR page unlisted and close it after the meet-up unless you allow publication on this website, and will mark the website's work pages so that search engines and AI crawlers are asked not to index or collect them. These measures lower the risk but cannot prevent it, and the organizers cannot control how others use copies they make.
    5. Integrity. The organizers will not alter the work. They may only scale it to fit a card or a screen, or show the opening lines of a text with a link to the full text.
    6. Attribution. The work will be credited to you by name or as "Anonymous," as selected above.
    7. Withdrawal. You may request removal of the work at any time by writing to hye-young.jo@colorado.edu. The work will then be removed from this website and excluded from future materials. Publications that have already appeared cannot be altered.

About

What is CHI?

CHI (the ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems) is the leading international conference on human-computer interaction. CHI 2027 takes place May 10–14 in Pittsburgh.

What is a meet-up?

Meet-ups are informal, 90-minute sessions where CHI attendees who share an interest gather for facilitated discussion. They are open to anyone registered for CHI, with no sign-up needed.

Our meet-up

We bring together researchers who build creativity tools and the creators who use them, to rethink what human creativity means as generative AI changes how we create. You can share your artwork even if you won't be at CHI.
